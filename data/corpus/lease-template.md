# Residential lease template

The lease template term is 12 months unless the parties write a different term. Rent is due on the first day of each month. The late fee in the template is $75 after the fifth day of the month. The security deposit in the template may not exceed one month of rent. The template says the landlord pays for structural repairs and the tenant pays for damage the tenant causes. Either party may end a month-to-month holdover with 30 days of written notice. This template is a form and not a decision about a dispute.
